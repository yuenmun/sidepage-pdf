Frequently asked questions

How do I add a PDF?

Tap Import PDF in the Library to choose one from the Files app. You can also share a PDF to Sidepage PDF from Mail, Safari or any other app.

How do I bookmark a page?

Tap the ribbon at the top of the page you’re reading. On iPhone Duo, the bookmarked page moves to the left side while you keep reading on the right.

Why do I only see one page?

Two panes appear when your iPhone Duo is open, on iOS 27.1 or later. When it’s folded, or on any other iPhone, Sidepage PDF shows one page at a time. Use the Reading | Bookmark control at the bottom to switch between them.

How do I read two pages like a book?

Open a PDF, tap the Mode menu and choose Two-Page Spread. Turn on Cover Page Alone if the spreads look out of step.

How do I jump to another page?

Tap the All Pages grid button: in the toolbar beside the pages when your iPhone Duo is open, or at the bottom right on a single screen. Tap a page to go there.

Can I change how it looks?

PDFs always show in their own colours, exactly as they were designed. For the app itself, choose System, Light or Dark in Settings or from the reading screen’s menu.

I deleted the sample PDFs. Can I get them back?

Go to Settings and tap Restore Sample PDFs.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Sidepage PDF works entirely on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
