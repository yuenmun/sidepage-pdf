Sidepage PDF

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Sidepage PDF does not collect any data. Your documents stay on your iPhone.

What Sidepage PDF stores

When you import a PDF, Sidepage PDF keeps a copy inside the app on your device, along with your bookmarks, reading position and settings. This information never leaves your iPhone. Deleting a PDF in Sidepage PDF, or deleting the app, removes it.

If you use iPhone or iCloud Backup, your device’s backup may include Sidepage PDF’s files. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy. We cannot see them.

What Sidepage PDF does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No network requests: Sidepage PDF does not send your documents or any information about you to us or anyone else.

Files you open

Sidepage PDF only reads the PDFs you choose to import from the Files app or share to it from another app. It does not access your photos, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it.

Children

Sidepage PDF is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
